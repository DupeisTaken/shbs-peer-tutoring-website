# Reviewing proposed changes

**Changes Awaiting Approval** explains which coordinator proposals have not yet taken effect. Approving applies a proposal; rejecting preserves the reason without applying it. Pending, approved, rejected, cancelled and all-state controls retain review history.

**Requested by** lists only people who have submitted a proposal. It shows their current readable identity, adding email for duplicate names, and preserves a historical name when an account no longer exists. Internal account IDs remain filter values and are not displayed as labels.

Existing reviewer permissions, self-review prevention, consequence confirmation and immutable request evidence remain enforced by the server.
