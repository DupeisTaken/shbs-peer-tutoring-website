# Tutor participation handbook

Draft for school review · 9 September 2026

## Tutoring and attendance

Use your assigned roster and report what actually happened. Current policy acceptance is required before submitting attendance. Management can correct historical records with a reason and audit snapshots; HEAD receives an in-system notification. Coordinator corrections take effect only after ADMIN or HEAD approval.

Planned room bookings cannot overlap within the same program period. Back-to-back bookings are allowed. If a past session actually occurred in a room with a recorded conflict, report it truthfully: the system warns you, records the conflict and notifies management. This does not authorize a future overlapping booking.

When a student's availability creates a schedule conflict, submit a reasoned schedule-rejection application. The current assignment continues until approved. Approval removes only that assignment and returns the student for matching without resetting their priority or verification deadline.

## Tutor meetings and service hours

Submit a meeting excuse at least 60 minutes before the meeting begins. Excused absences do not consume the unexcused-absence allowance.

Q1 and Q2 form semester one; Q3 and Q4 form semester two within each recorded school year. The first three unexcused tutor-meeting absences in a semester have no automatic deduction. Each later unexcused absence deducts 0.25 service hours. This replaces the former 0.125-hour rule. Corrections recalculate later deductions in the same semester; manual adjustments remain separate.

Completed interviews earn their actual recorded duration for attendees. Scheduling an interview does not earn hours. Management records completion, duration and attendance with a reason; a correction replaces earlier automatic credits rather than adding a second credit.

## Interview panels and decisions

A panel needs at least three distinct active tutors with accounts, at least one management member, and at least one staff-confirmed tutor qualified in an applicant subject.

A highest-ranking management panelist chairs the interview: HEAD outranks ADMIN, which outranks COORDINATOR. If multiple panelists share the highest rank, the selected chair resolves ties.

Every panelist must vote before a final decision. The majority determines the result; an evenly split panel is decided by the chair. A coordinator chair submits that outcome for ADMIN or HEAD approval. The reviewer authorizes the chair's proposed outcome without replacing the chair's identity or vote. Changed panel evidence requires a fresh proposal.

## Feedback, messages and publishing

Student feedback is staff-only unless management enables sharing with the session's tutor. Access can be revoked for both existing and future feedback. Private messages are visible only to their two participants.

Assigned translators prepare drafts. Publication requires management review; coordinators also need ADMIN or HEAD approval. Translator status does not grant structural editing, deletion or account-management powers.
