# Student participation handbook

Repository revision 2026.09.09 · School publication review required

## Applying and confirming your account

Submit your subject choices, availability, contact details and signed policy agreement when intake opens. Your first submitted survey sets your priority; creating an account earlier does not improve it. Repeated submissions preserve the original request and timestamp.

Use the emailed link to confirm your request and create your password. If you already have an account, confirmation keeps your existing role and password. Each link lasts 24 hours. A successful resend replaces the old link without changing your priority.

Staff may assign a tutor before you confirm. Your first assignment starts a fixed seven-day verification deadline. Reassignment and email resends do not extend it. At the deadline, an unverified request is permanently disqualified and its assignments are removed. You may submit a fresh request with a new priority timestamp.

## Managing participation

After confirmation, sign in to view assignments and edit availability. An availability edit preserves your subject choices, priority and current assignments. If a tutor cannot accommodate it, they may request review; an approved schedule rejection removes only that tutor's assignment and returns the request for matching at its original priority.

You can recall an unassigned request after confirming the consequences. Recall permanently closes that request; a fresh request receives a new timestamp. Once assigned, apply to withdraw for the quarter. Assignments continue while the application is pending or if it is denied. Approval removes all assignments and prevents another request in the same quarter, including after an account email change.

Important request actions require reading a consequence dialog and waiting five seconds before confirmation. A coordinator's management changes require ADMIN or HEAD approval before they take effect.

## Your records and support

Attend agreed sessions, prepare the material you need help with, communicate absences promptly and treat others respectfully. Report incorrect attendance to management with the relevant date and session. Do not submit another participant's private information in a public bug report.

Only valid disciplinary cards count toward standing: three yellow cards count as one effective red card, and two effective red cards reach the removal-review threshold. Reaching that threshold does not automatically delete your history. Pending or invalid cards are excluded from the count; a pending appeal leaves the existing card's validity unchanged until reviewed.

Your attendance, feedback and disciplinary appeal history remain available across program refreshes and new intakes. Your account settings, history, feedback, appeals and private messages remain accessible while policy renewal is pending.

Feedback is visible only to management by default. Management can enable or revoke sharing with the tutor responsible for the session; the setting also applies to existing feedback. The portal states the current visibility.

You may appeal your own disciplinary card within five school days. Weekdays count unless the school calendar specifies a holiday or make-up day. A pending appeal does not automatically invalidate the card. An upheld appeal invalidates it and recalculates disciplinary standing; management corrections retain reasons and audit evidence.

You can exchange private in-system messages with management. Only the two participants can read a conversation. Notifications do not include message bodies.

## Policy changes and account security

Before further participation, read and accept any revised policy. Renewal requires a ten-second confirmation and preserves the exact accepted text, signature and time. It does not change your original request priority. Change your login email through verified account settings; editing contact information cannot substitute for verification.
