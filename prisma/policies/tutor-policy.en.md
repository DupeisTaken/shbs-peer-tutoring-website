# Sample tutor participation policy

Sample revision 2026.09.21 · Adapt and approve before publication

This is a sample policy for the peer-tutoring website, not an approved school policy. The school must review the wording, select its program modules, and add its contact details and effective date before publishing. “Management” includes coordinators, administrators (ADMIN) and the program leader (HEAD). Sensitive coordinator changes require ADMIN or HEAD approval.

Feature-specific sections apply where the school has enabled those workflows. Account role, tutor participation, crew membership and translator assignment are separate permissions.

## Joining, leaving and returning

Apply through **Become a Tutor** with your subject choices, qualifications, contact details and policy agreement. Application review and interview come before acceptance. Use the issued registration code or setup link to verify your email and establish account access; an application or roster entry alone is not a login. Tutor participation requires an explicitly linked tutor profile, even for management accounts.

An active tutor can request opt-out. The tutor remains active until staff approve it, and approval cannot happen until the seven-day waiting period has elapsed. You may recall a pending request. An opted-out tutor can request reentry without a new waiting period, but must still wait for approval. Only one pending membership request is allowed. Staff must explicitly requeue affected students after a tutor opts out.

When a program refresh crosses a semester boundary, continuing active tutors become pending and must choose whether they are available or opting out. Past attendance and hours remain recorded. Quarter mode graduates active tutors in grade 12 or above on entry to Q4; semester mode does so at the school-year boundary. Other continuing active tutors with recorded grades advance one grade at the school-year boundary. Ask management to correct inaccurate profile information before refresh.

## Scheduling and attendance

Maintain your available times and coordinate sessions with assigned tutees. Planned room bookings cannot overlap within one program period or conflict with a room blackout; back-to-back bookings are allowed. Clearing a pairing's default-slot link retains its copied day and time. A later timezone change preserves weekly wall-clock slots and saved appointment instants; check the displayed program timezone.

Record the actual date, start/end time, room or online status, your own attendance, and every student's attendance. Future school dates cannot be submitted. Include comments, a reason for your own absence, and reasons for excused student absences. Held sessions with a present student also require the displayed ratings. Present, rescheduled and extra sessions use the same credit calculation. An online session has no physical room check.

When several assigned subjects run in one combined block, use the combined attendance form. Shared time and distinct present students count once, not once per subject. A retry of the same submission does not earn duplicate credit. Ask management to correct a saved entry instead of resubmitting changed answers for that block.

Report a completed session truthfully even if its actual room conflicts with a recorded booking. The website warns and notifies management; it does not permit a future conflicting reservation. Historical corrections require a reason and audit evidence, notify HEAD, and can recalculate hours, discipline, pairings and attendance-review decisions. Coordinator corrections require approval.

## Schedule conflicts and student withdrawal

If a tutee's availability cannot work, submit a reasoned schedule-rejection request. The current assignment continues until approval. Approval removes only the affected pairing and returns the request for matching without resetting priority or an existing verification deadline.

Survey tutees request whole-period withdrawal from their own account. For enrollments offering a tutor-relayed opt-out, relay only the tutee's actual request: it remains recallable for seven days and is processed automatically after that window unless recalled or cancelled by staff. Neither route is a substitute for reporting a schedule conflict.

## Service hours

A held session earns program-rounded duration multiplied by **1 + the number of distinct students recorded PRESENT**. An unexcused absent student does not increase the multiplier but leaves the baseline tutor credit. If the tutor is absent, or nobody is present and nobody is unexcused absent, session credit is zero. Excused absence is not present attendance.

To round, take the minutes remaining after complete hours. If that remainder is at most 10 minutes, round the total duration down to a half-hour step; otherwise round it up to a half-hour step. Apply the attendance multiplier after rounding.

**Recorded minutes → rounded hours before multiplier**

- 10 → 0
- 11 → 0.5
- 30 → 0.5
- 35 → 1
- 60 → 1
- 70 → 1
- 71 → 1.5
- 90 → 1.5

For example, 35 minutes with two present students earns 1 × (1 + 2) = 3 service hours. A held 60-minute session with only an unexcused absent student earns the baseline 1 hour. These are service-credit units, not elapsed clock hours. Review the dashboard and period totals; authorized manual extras and deductions are recorded separately.

## Meetings and interview credit

Submit or cancel your own meeting excuse no later than 60 minutes before the meeting. Staff record meeting attendance. Excused absences do not use the unexcused-absence allowance.

Q1/Q2 form semester one and Q3/Q4 semester two within a school year. The first three unexcused meeting absences in a semester have no automatic deduction. Each later one deducts 0.25 service hours. Corrections recalculate those deductions; manual adjustments remain separate.

Completed interviews credit actual recorded minutes divided by 60 to the recorded attendees. Scheduling alone earns nothing. Management records completion, duration and attendees with a reason; a correction replaces the earlier automatic credit.

## Interview panels and decisions

A panel requires at least three distinct active tutors with accounts, at least one management member, and staff-confirmed qualification coverage for an applicant subject. The highest-ranking management panelist chairs it: HEAD, then ADMIN, then COORDINATOR. When several share the highest rank, one is selected as chair.

Every panelist must vote before a final decision. The majority decides; the chair resolves a tie. A coordinator chair submits the chosen outcome for ADMIN or HEAD approval, preserving the chair's authorship. If the panel or other review evidence changes, prepare a fresh proposal. Only the current chair can schedule the interview.

## Discipline and attendance review

Prepare suitable materials, communicate absences promptly and keep records accurate. Request a yellow or red student card only with a reason. Tutor-requested cards stay pending until management validates them. With discipline enabled, an unexcused student absence automatically creates a valid red card.

Every 3 valid yellow cards count as 1 effective red card. At 2 effective red cards, an active tutee is automatically made inactive and removed from current-period pairings. This does not require a separate removal approval. Pending or invalid cards do not count; a pending appeal does not suspend a valid card. Students may appeal within five school days. Successful appeals and corrections recalculate standing and restore only eligible effects of the removed card; accounts and past records are retained. Tutors see only the disciplinary details authorized for their roster.

Crew observations may flag an in-person session when the observed student count is lower than recorded attendance. A flag is a request for review, not an automatic penalty. Management may dismiss it, warn, apply a recorded hour deduction or escalate for review. An escalation alone does not automatically remove a tutor. Corrected attendance or patrol evidence can reopen the review and remove its linked deduction.

## Crew, announcements and additional permissions

Tutor status does not grant crew membership. If you also have active crew membership, record each visited room once with the actual student headcount and observation time. A submitted patrol with at least one room observation earns 0.5 crew service hours, recorded separately. Repeating the same submission does not add credit. Crew opt-out requires a seven-day wait and staff approval; reentry requires approval without that wait.

Read announcements addressed to your linked tutor identity and use the acknowledgement control when available. Announcements use a fixed published audience; they are not private conversations. Assigned translators may submit text drafts, but publication needs management review. Extra translator or crew access does not grant account administration or unrestricted management writes.

## Feedback and messages

Student session feedback is management-only by default. Management may share it with the responsible tutor or revoke sharing, including for past feedback.

Messages send immediately to at most 20 permitted recipients as separate deliveries. Recipients cannot see the other recipients or replies. Contact groups and user overrides govern both new messages and replies; if a selected recipient becomes ineligible before sending, the whole send is rejected. Retrying the same send does not duplicate deliveries. Losing contact eligibility preserves readable history; messaging restrictions prevent new sends and incoming eligibility.

HEAD and ADMIN may review messages composed with the supervision disclosure, record a reason, hide or restore content, and restrict messaging. Coordinators cannot supervise other conversations. Hidden content is retained for authorized review. Participant-only messages remain outside supervision; a new disclosed reply follows its own rules. Notifications omit message bodies and recipient lists. Keep participant information out of public bug reports.

## Policy acceptance and account security

Current tutor-policy acceptance is required to submit attendance. Published changes prompt renewed review on navigation or window focus. Scroll through the policy, explicitly check agreement and complete the ten-second confirmation. Both tutor and tutee policies apply if you have both linked profiles. The exact accepted revision, text, signature and time remain recorded; missing translations fall back to English.

You can dismiss a renewal prompt and still use account settings, history, feedback, appeals and messages, subject to ordinary access rules. Changing a contact field is not email verification: use the verified account email-change flow. Keep codes and account links private, use password recovery when needed, and configure personal email two-factor authentication when the program enables it and delivery is available. Suspension blocks ordinary access; the suspension page offers an account appeal, which does not restore access while pending.
