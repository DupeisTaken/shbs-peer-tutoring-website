# SHBS Peer Tutoring Tutor Policy

**Revision:** 2026.09.24 | **Status:** Draft for school review

This policy establishes expectations, responsibilities and procedures for tutors in the SHBS Peer Tutoring Program. It retains the structure of the October 2025 policy and incorporates the program website.

This draft takes effect only after school approval and publication with an effective date and a confirmed management contact. Until then, follow the school's currently published policy. “Management” means coordinators, administrators (ADMIN) and the program leader (HEAD). Sensitive coordinator actions require ADMIN or HEAD approval. Optional-module rules apply when the relevant module is enabled. Dates and deadlines use the displayed program timezone.

## I. Tutor Responsibilities

### 1. Course materials and preparation

Request materials in advance and prepare a structured session suited to each tutee's needs. Encourage tutees to provide materials at least 48 hours beforehand where possible. Help them understand and practise the work, and treat their questions and personal information respectfully.

### 2. Reminders and session delivery

Send reminders at least 24 hours before sessions through an available contact route. Teach punctually and professionally. Keep your subject willingness and availability current, and agree the regular time and location with assigned tutees. **Awaiting schedule** means a slot still needs confirmation. Qualification approval and willingness to teach are separate; changing willingness does not grant a qualification.

### 3. Attendance documentation

Submit attendance through the **Tutor Dashboard** within 24 hours of each session. This is a program expectation, not an automatic website deadline or penalty. Falsification is prohibited. Record the actual date, start/end times, room or online status, your attendance and each tutee's attendance. Include required comments, absence reasons and ratings. Future school dates cannot be submitted.

For one block covering several assigned subjects, use combined attendance: shared time and distinct present tutees count once. Do not create another entry to change a saved block. Ask management for a correction with the session and error details. Corrections retain reasons and audit evidence and may recalculate hours, cards and assignments.

## II. Attendance Policy

### 1. Notification and rescheduling

Tutors and tutees should notify each other at least 24 hours before an absence. In an emergency, communicate as soon as possible and explain the circumstances. Prioritize agreed rescheduling over cancellation and record what actually happened. If website messaging is unavailable, use the school's program contact route.

### 2. Tardiness and absence classification

A tutee arriving 15 minutes or more late without notice is normally recorded as unexcused absent under this policy. Discuss an explained emergency with management before finalizing a disputed classification. The website does not time arrivals: record the facts accurately. The unexcused-absence record triggers Section VI; lateness does not create a second automatic card.

### 3. Scheduling and conflicts

Check the program timezone. Planned room bookings cannot overlap another pairing or a recurring room block; back-to-back sessions are allowed. Online sessions need no physical room check. If the actual room used conflicts with a booking, report the completed session truthfully; the website warns management.

If a tutee's availability cannot work, submit a reasoned schedule-review request. The assignment continues until approval. Approval removes only the affected assignment and returns the request for matching without resetting its original priority or verification deadline.

### 4. Leaving or returning

Request tutor opt-out through your account. You remain active until staff approval, which cannot occur until the seven-day waiting period has elapsed. You may recall a pending request. Reentry after opt-out needs approval but no new waiting period. Only one membership request may be pending. Management must explicitly requeue affected tutees.

Tutees with the website withdrawal option request withdrawal themselves. For an enrollment offering tutor-relayed opt-out, relay only the tutee's actual request: it is recallable for seven days, then processed automatically unless recalled or cancelled. If the period refreshes or its originating assignment is removed first, the outdated relay is cancelled. These routes do not replace schedule review.

## III. Service Hours Accrual

### 1. Earning hours

For a held session, **service hours = program-rounded recorded duration × (1 + distinct tutees marked PRESENT)**. The baseline represents preparation and tutor time; additional units reflect attending tutees. These are service-credit units, not elapsed clock hours. Present, rescheduled and extra sessions use the same formula.

An unexcused absent tutee does not increase the multiplier but preserves baseline credit. Credit is zero if the tutor is absent, or if no tutee is present and none is unexcused absent. Excused absence is not present attendance. Identical retries do not earn duplicate credit.

### 2. Calculation and rounding

Round duration before applying the multiplier. Take the minutes remaining after complete hours: if the remainder is at most 10 minutes, round the total duration down to a half-hour step; otherwise round it up to a half-hour step.

| Recorded minutes | Rounded hours before multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| 10 | 0 |
| 11 | 0.5 |
| 30 | 0.5 |
| 35 | 1 |
| 60 | 1 |
| 70 | 1 |
| 71 | 1.5 |
| 90 | 1.5 |

For example, 35 minutes with two present tutees earns 1 × (1 + 2) = 3 hours. A held 60-minute session with only an unexcused absent tutee earns 1 hour. A 60-minute session with all tutees excused absent earns zero.

### 3. Interviews and other credits

Completed interviews credit actual recorded minutes divided by 60 to recorded attendees. Scheduling alone earns nothing. Management records completion, duration and attendees; corrections replace earlier automatic credits. Authorized extra hours and deductions appear separately in period totals.

Tutor status does not grant crew membership. An active crew member's submitted patrol with at least one room observation earns 0.5 crew hours separately from tutoring. Record actual observation times and headcounts; retries do not add credit. Crew opt-out requires a seven-day wait and approval; reentry requires approval without that wait.

## IV. Policy Enforcement and Penalties

### 1. Attendance expectations and tutor meetings

Attend assigned sessions and meetings and raise recurring problems promptly. Follow published meeting dates; a fixed Monday lunch meeting is not assumed. Submit or cancel your own meeting excuse on the website no later than 60 minutes before the meeting. Staff record attendance and correct staff-entered statuses.

### 2. Meeting deductions

The first three unexcused meeting absences in a semester have no automatic deduction. Each subsequent unexcused meeting absence deducts 0.25 service hours. Excused absences do not consume this allowance. In quarter mode, Q1/Q2 form semester one and Q3/Q4 semester two of the same school year. Corrections recalculate automatic deductions.

This revision does not carry forward the legacy blanket three-absence limit, automatic 1-hour tutor-session deduction or 0.125-hour meeting deduction. Any separate disciplinary adjustment requires an authorized management decision and a recorded reason; manual adjustments remain separate from automatic meeting deductions.

### 3. Attendance review and corrections

Crew observations may flag an in-person session when observed headcount is below recorded attendance. A flag requests review; it is not itself a penalty. Management may dismiss it, warn, record an hour deduction or escalate for review. Escalation alone does not remove a tutor. Attendance or patrol corrections can reopen review and remove its linked deduction. Coordinator decisions and historical corrections require approval where applicable.

## V. Tutee Disciplinary Guidelines

### 1. Yellow and red cards

When discipline is enabled, tutors may request a yellow card for a response delayed beyond 24 hours, incomplete agreed work or other documented minor conduct issues, and a red card for serious disrespect. Explain the circumstances and avoid duplicating a card already created for the same attendance incident. Requests remain pending until management validates them; a request or comment alone is not a valid card.

Only valid cards count. Every 3 valid yellow cards count as 1 effective red card. At 2 effective red cards, the website automatically makes an active tutee inactive and removes current-period assignments without a separate removal approval. Pending and invalid cards do not count. The account and past records remain.

### 2. Reporting and appeals

Every card needs a written reason. Tutees may appeal a card once through **Support**, by the end of the fifth school day after its recorded issue date. School days follow the program timezone and staff-maintained holidays and make-up days. A pending appeal does not suspend the card or its effects.

An upheld appeal invalidates the card and recalculates standing. Corrections can restore eligible assignments removed by that disciplinary action if no later independent status change prevents restoration; restoration of every former assignment is not guaranteed. Refer disputed records and expired appeal windows to management. Access only disciplinary information authorized for your roster.

## VI. Automatic Cards Issuance

With discipline enabled, a recorded **unexcused tutee absence automatically creates 1 valid red card**. Record excused absences and reasons accurately. The website does not automatically issue a card for every three excused absences or issue a separate lateness card from a timer.

Management reviews requested cards and corrections. Attendance corrections may change cards and disciplinary consequences; report errors instead of adding compensating attendance entries or duplicate cards.

## VII. Tutor Recruitments

### 1. Application and account setup

Apply through **Become a Tutor** during tutor recruitment. Complete the displayed required questions and accept the published tutor policy. Tutor and tutee recruitment windows are independent; a closed or paused form is viewable but cannot accept submissions. The application records its accepted policy revision and text.

Review and, where enabled, interview precede admission. After acceptance, use the issued registration code or setup link and verify your email. An application or roster row alone is not a login. Participation requires a linked tutor profile with tutoring access, including for management accounts.

### 2. Interview procedure

When interviews are enabled, the panel has at least **three distinct active tutors with accounts**, including at least one management member and staff-confirmed qualification coverage for an applicant subject. The highest-ranking management panelist chairs: HEAD, then ADMIN, then COORDINATOR; select one chair if ranks tie. Only the current chair schedules the interview.

Retain the teaching demonstration format: the team supplies a topic at least 24 hours beforehand and normally allows 15–20 minutes for the demonstration. These are organizational expectations, not automatic website timers. Confirm arrangements with the chair.

Every panelist must vote before a final decision. A simple majority decides; the chair resolves a tie. A coordinator chair submits the chosen outcome for ADMIN or HEAD approval. A pending proposal is not admission. Changed panel or review evidence requires a fresh proposal.

### 3. Qualifications and continued participation

Use the additional-subject or higher-level qualification application where available. Approval records eligibility; willingness and timetable availability remain separate and do not guarantee assignments.

At a refresh crossing a semester boundary, continuing active tutors become pending and must confirm availability or opt out. Quarter mode graduates active tutors in grade 12 or above on entry to Q4; semester mode does so at the school-year boundary. Other continuing active tutors with recorded grades advance one grade at that boundary. Check profile details before refresh. Attendance and hours remain in history.

## VIII. Communication Protocols

### 1. Team contact and notices

Read addressed announcements and acknowledge them when prompted. Report recurring absences, learning difficulties and record errors through website messages or the school's program contact. Announcements have a fixed published audience. Optional email notices depend on program settings and personal preferences; do not rely solely on email reminders. Security notices are independent of optional notification preferences.

### 2. Messages, feedback and privacy

Messages send immediately to up to 20 permitted recipients as separate deliveries; recipients cannot see others or their replies. Replies use current permissions. If any selected recipient becomes ineligible before sending, the entire send is rejected. Identical retries do not duplicate deliveries. Contact changes retain readable history; messaging restrictions block new sends and incoming eligibility.

Read the composer's supervision disclosure. HEAD and ADMIN may review disclosed messages with a recorded reason, hide or restore content, and restrict messaging. Coordinators cannot supervise other people's conversations. Hidden content is retained for authorized review. Participant-only messages remain outside that supervision workflow; a newly disclosed reply follows its own rules. Notifications omit message bodies and recipient lists. Private messaging is not end-to-end encrypted.

Session feedback is management-only by default. Management may share it with the responsible tutor or revoke sharing, including for earlier feedback. The website's **Privacy policy** explains information handling separately from this agreement. Keep names, private messages, contact details, codes and account links out of public bug reports.

### 3. Policy acceptance and account security

Read and explicitly accept the published policy. A published change prompts renewed review on a visit or window focus: read to the end, check agreement and complete the ten-second confirmation. Accounts linked to both tutor and tutee profiles review both policies. Missing translations fall back to English. Acceptance preserves the exact revision, text, signature and time; unpublished proposals do not renew consent.

Current tutor-policy acceptance is required to submit attendance. Dismissing a renewal prompt preserves access to history, feedback, appeals, messages and account settings, subject to ordinary permissions. It does not permit participation requiring current acceptance.

Keep passwords, codes and account links private. Change login email through verified account settings; editing a contact field does not verify it. Secondary email is optional and subject to the program's binding setting. Use password recovery as needed and email two-factor authentication when enabled and available. Suspension blocks ordinary access; its appeal page does not restore access while an appeal is pending.

By explicitly accepting the school's published version, tutors agree to these responsibilities and procedures. Contact management with questions before accepting.
